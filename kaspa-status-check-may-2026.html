Kaspa status check - May 2026

Toccata activated at DAA score 474,165,565. This page started as a May snapshot and picked up a June 29 pass: fresh mainnet numbers, the Toccata release record, and the claims whose wording was too loose.

Last checked: June 30, 2026. Re-run this check at emission step-downs, after new release or KIP activity, or when app and tooling evidence changes.

Snapshot

What is live now

Base network

Proof of Work, UTXO ownership, blockDAG structure, GHOSTDAG ordering, and 10 BPS mainnet blocks since the Crescendo upgrade.

L1 readout

The June 29 public REST check returned kaspa-mainnet, virtual DAA 473,005,279, 1,102,009 blocks, and roughly 27.542 billion KAS in circulation.

Issuance

The block-reward endpoint returned 2.59565436 KAS per block that day. The published emission schedule steps that number down every month.

What changed

New source notes to add

Official framing

The redesigned Kaspa.org now leads with real-time decentralization as its north star, ahead of throughput or price framing.

Roadmap order

Kaspa.org sequences Toccata first, DAGKnight next, then a separate proposed 2027 hard-fork bucket for 100 BPS and partition-resilient payments.

Toccata release

Rusty Kaspa v2.0.1 is the current Toccata build. v2.0.0 did the activation: DAA score 474,165,565, roughly June 30, 2026 at 16:15 UTC.

KIP alignment

The June 29 raw KIP check lists KIP-16 and KIP-20 as proposed and implemented, active on TN10; KIP-17 and KIP-21 as implemented and active on TN10. That's testnet and design evidence; mainnet activation is checked separately.

Open KIP PRs

KIP-24 adds transaction-v1 fields for compute budgets, covenant bindings, user lanes, and ZK-friendly txids. KIP-22 proposes P2MR ScriptPublicKey work for quantum resistance and MAST-style scripts. Both are still open pull requests, not merged code.

TN10 activation tests

Rusty Kaspa's tn10-toc2 pre-release set a Testnet-10 activation point at DAA score 467,579,632; tn10-toc3 set final Toccata ZK hardening at DAA 476,232,000. A June 29 Testnet-10 REST check showed virtual DAA 503,616,667: testnet evidence, not a mainnet claim.

Argent

Michael Sutton's Argent repo is early actor-style Silverscript tooling. It's prototype research: not audited, not stable, not evidence of mainnet activation.

Builder tooling

Python SDK v2.0.0 and v2.0.1 added Toccata-aligned wallet, lane-proof, SMT-sync, and covenant-binding support. Rusty Kaspa's merged ZK SDK PR adds an R0ScriptBuilder helper for RISC Zero proof scripts.

Emission check

The published schedule: 27.5 KAS/sec from May 8, 25.9565436 from June 7, 24.49971475 from July 7. It's a step-down, not a cliff: the schedule has no drop-off point.

Claims corrected

Wording fixes this month

Instant finality

There's no instant finality claim to make. Blocks include fast; confirmation confidence builds probabilistically after that.

Toccata activation

The protocol activated at DAA 474,165,565. Wallet, explorer, and app support are separate claims with their own status.

Emission cliff

Emission steps down monthly against the published schedule. There's no cliff to fall off.

DAGKnight live

It's research and an early prototype, not a mainnet consensus change. Current behavior on mainnet needs its own activation evidence.

100 BPS live

10 BPS is what's running. 100 BPS sits in the proposed 2027 hard-fork bucket, unbuilt.

KIP evidence

KIPs merged and active on TN10 are testnet and design evidence. Mainnet activation needs its own activation record and observed behavior.

One API is proof

A public API read is a snapshot, not a status change. Confirm changes against releases, KIPs, node behavior, and accepted mainnet blocks.
