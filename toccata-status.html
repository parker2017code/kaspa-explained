Toccata status

Kaspa Toccata status.

Toccata is the hard-fork track for covenant-style Kaspa L1 programmability. As of June 4, 2026, public sources show TN12 testnet work and Testnet-10 past the tn10-toc2 activation score of 467,579,632 and the tn10-toc3 hardening score of 476,232,000. Mainnet activation still needs mainnet evidence.

Citable summary: Toccata targets Kaspa L1 covenants, ZK proof hooks, sequencing commitments, and based-app foundations; call it pending until release and mainnet activation evidence exists.

Wording

Use this wording.

As of June 4, 2026, Toccata should be described as a targeted hard-fork track with TN12 testnet work, Testnet-10 activation and hardening evidence, TN10 KIP implementation evidence, and no mainnet activation claim. Mainnet claims need mainnet release and activation evidence.
What it can enable

Vault rules, asset rules, escrow paths, assurance contracts, proof checks, sequencing commitments, and standalone based-app foundations.

What it does not prove yet

Mature native DeFi, full vProgs, full app-to-app atomic composition, or mainnet production readiness.

What to verify next

Post-test stability, TN12 status, branch merge, audit/rehearsal, mainnet release notes, activation parameters, and upgraded-node behavior.
