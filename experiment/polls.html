TN10 Polls

On-chain polls on Testnet-10. Anyone can create a poll (a question plus 2-4 options) and vote once per poll per wallet. Every poll and every vote is a real TN10 transaction to one shared, well-known board address. Results are tallied live by reading and parsing that address's transaction history, no backend or database involved.
