TN10 Tip Jar

Send a small tip and a short message to one shared Testnet-10 address, and see who else has tipped. Runs entirely client-side against the official Rusty Kaspa WASM SDK (v2.0.1) and public TN10 infrastructure. No backend, no custody, nothing leaves your browser except signed transactions.
