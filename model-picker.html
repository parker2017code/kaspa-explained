How the score works
  1. Each dial is a weight. Turn one up and it takes a bigger share. The shares beside the dials add to 100.
  2. A missing figure does not zero a dial. A model missing one of a dial's figures is scored on the others behind it, and drops out of the ranking only once too little of what you asked about was ever measured on it.
  3. Zero is the worst result anyone has recorded on a figure, 100 the best. It is not a percentile across these 23 models, which would move every score whenever a model was added.
  4. Cost is not part of the score. It is the horizontal axis of the value chart below and nothing else, so a cheap model cannot earn credit twice.
  5. Four boards, ten figures. Four from Artificial Analysis, four from LiveBench, one from LM Arena, and ARC-AGI-2 from ARC Prize, all read 25 August 2026. Every figure is a published benchmark on its own; no board's own overall score feeds anything.
  6. It can tell you a model is at the frontier. It is much weaker at telling you which frontier model is best, because how you weight the dials moves the answer further than which models are on the list.

Every decision behind that, in full: why six dials and not ten, how each model's effort setting is chosen, how a figure no board published gets estimated, and what was tested and rejected.
