Finality

Fast is not final.

Your transaction can land in a block before you finish reading this sentence. It still isn't irreversible in that moment: reversal risk drops as more proof-of-work piles on top.

Summary: Kaspa has fast mined inclusion and confirmation confidence that rises with accumulated work. "Real-time" describes inclusion speed, not finality.

Payment path

Five steps from broadcast to trusted

1Seen

Your wallet broadcasts the transaction and nodes pick it up.

2Included

A miner mines it into a proof-of-work block.

3Ordered

The block joins the DAG alongside blocks mined in parallel, and GHOSTDAG orders the history.

4Accepted

Nodes accept the ordered transaction under Kaspa's consensus rules.

5Confidence

Reversal risk keeps falling as more blocks stack proof-of-work on top of it.

The claim

How to say it right

Kaspa's 10 BPS blockDAG and GHOSTDAG ordering cut payment latency: a transaction can be mined into a block in a fraction of a second. Confirmation confidence still rises probabilistically as more work accumulates on top of it.
ShortcutSafer version
Kaspa payments are irreversible immediately.Inclusion is fast; reversal risk drops as confirmation confidence rises.
10 BPS means 10x finality.10 BPS is live and speeds up inclusion. Finality confidence still depends on accumulated proof-of-work and network conditions.
Real-time means irreversible now.Real-time describes how fast a block gets mined, not how final the transaction inside it is.
