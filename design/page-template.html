The reasoning

Why DAA score instead of wall-clock time

Block production rate varies with network conditions even under a target BPS, so a schedule keyed to elapsed real time would drift against actual chain progress. Keying to DAA score ties each step to a count of accepted blocks instead, which is exact and reproducible from the chain itself rather than from a clock.
