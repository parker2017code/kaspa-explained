Kaspa builder review queue

Builder and investor interest can create bad matches when it goes public too early. Submissions start private, get reviewed, then become opt-in cards only when the people involved agree.

Status: payments are live today. Toccata app-layer rules need wallet support, tooling support, and app receipts.

Board rules

  1. Private first. Raw submissions stay private until the founder opts into a public card.
  2. Public card. Cards show idea, user, Kaspa fit, stage, evidence, help needed, and contact rule.
  3. Score meaning. The score measures review readiness. Financial quality requires diligence outside this worksheet.
  4. Intro consent. Introductions happen only when both sides opt in.
  5. Status labels. Live, testnet, targeted, roadmap, and research labels stay visible.

Review loop

1. Classify

Place each submission in live, Toccata, based-app, or research lane.

2. Redact

Remove private names, email, city, and company data unless public sharing is approved.

3. Score

Rate user, Kaspa fit, evidence, risk, distribution, and next action.

4. Draft

Create a short public card and a private intro note.

5. Review

A human checks claims and consent before publishing or sending intros.

6. Route

Share approved cards or intros only where the submitter agreed.
