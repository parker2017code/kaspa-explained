# Research findings, 22 August 2026

Two agents, verified against primary sources. This is the raw material for the
post rewrite and the page corrections. Nothing here is written up yet.

## The story this cycle

The protocol layer is quiet. No node release since v2.0.1 on 15 June. No
security incident found, checked explicitly. All the actual argument is
happening one layer up, on kas-smiths.org, where five named people are fighting
out the shape of Kaspa's first fungible token covenant standard in public with
real numbers on the table.

## Verified, usable

**Toccata activated on mainnet at DAA score 474,165,565, about 30 June 2026,
16:15 UTC**, per the v2.0.0 release body. Current release v2.0.1, 15 June.
Six "Toccata Cleanup" PRs merged 19 to 21 August, removing pre-Toccata code
paths and making protocol version 10 unconditional. One commit activated
Toccata on devnet, which is cleanup behind a fork already live on mainnet.

**kaspa.org has been wrong for eight weeks.** Its /developments and /build
pages still say Toccata is "currently live on TN12 ahead of mainnet
activation". Confirmed by browser, not just fetch. Also caught on 11 July and
never fixed. Source demoted in AGENTS.md, owner instruction 22 August.

**KCC reality is far thinner than the name suggests.** Only kcc-0001, kcc-0002
and kcc-0020 are merged, and all three say "Status: Draft" in their own body.
The repo has published no releases at all, so even those exist only as merged
markdown on main. KCC-0021 is open PR #6. KCC-0402 is open PR #4. Both PR
bodies claim production deployment, neither confirmed.

**KCC-0020 has an admitted, unresolved correctness bug.** Issue #14, opened 21
August by Knitser: the standard transfer consolidation rule and the extended
state update entrypoint contradict each other, permanently partitioning a
token's UTXO set if both are used. A KCC-0020 collaborator and Michael Sutton
both confirmed the problem is real in the thread. No fix has landed. So the
merged standard is three weeks old and known broken.

**The one production implementer disclaims conformance.** Issue #11: KaspaCom
states its deployed KCC20V2 is "partially aligned, not fully conforming" with
KCC-0001, 0002, 0020 and 0021. That directly undercuts PR #6's claim of
adoption "in production by kascov and KaspaRocket".

**The "18 KCC numbers in the pipeline" is one rejected submitter.** Every bulk
proposal, PRs #7, #8, #9, #10 and #12, came from a single external author and
every one closed unmerged. The specs that landed came from authors closer to
the core team. A pipeline of numbers is not consensus in progress.

**Michael Sutton on KCC-0020, 21 August:** "this is in good state now, so this
will be a good time for reference impls and experimenting." The author's own
framing is experiment stage, not production.

**Standardization is fiction on-chain so far.** 0xKnitser, who runs kascov,
posted TN10 data: 867,456 spent covenant sigscripts reduce to under 3,000
template reveals and exactly 3 distinct templates. On mainnet one launchpad's
74 live tokens already span four template generations. Old shapes never retire
and there is no migration path except opt-in burn and reissue.

**The design fight had numbers.** Shawn, an independent covenant deployer who
commissioned a third-party audit of his production covenant set, argued 9 to 11
August that KCC-0020's three-entrypoint design bloats compiled bytecode about
1.8x and breaks composability limits. He pushed a two-entrypoint fold with
borrow as a signature-less branch. A concrete compiled-size tradeoff, not a
style opinion.

**Kaspa x402 reached alpha.10 on 10 August**, by elldeeone (Luke Dunshea).
Demo at demo.kaspa-x402.org, testing on testnet-10, intent to submit upstream
to the x402 standard once release-candidate ready. Reviewed by Ross, IzioDev,
Brociferous and VidaWallet.

**A claim that is now known false:** elldeeone corrected his own earlier
statement that Kaspa was the first UTXO chain to support x402. Cardano was
first. If any page or draft implies Kaspa was first, that is wrong.

**KCC-0021 metadata revised 15 August** by 0xKnitser, splitting metadata into
immutable claimed_ fields from genesis and mutable listed_ fields from signed
token lists, after image hosting failures argued against pure immutability.

**KCC721 NFT covenant draft**, 3 August, by KasBTC (HUB21). Atomic batch
transfers cap around 22 NFTs per transaction against Kaspa's compute mass
limit. One reply. Early.

**KIPs:** the Toccata bundle, 16, 17, 20, 21, are Active and mainnet
activated, the only items in the whole survey clearing every state. Two open
drafts moved: PR #39 multisig wallet conventions through 17 August, PR #44
KIP-0012 browser extension wallet APIs through 10 August.

**vProgs:** no activity since 28 July. Research, matches the site's label.
**argent-lang:** three repos pushed 17 August, README says not release ready,
gated on a pending Silverscript audit. Pre-alpha.
**Silverscript:** README says experimental, no releases, testnet-10 only.

## Unverified, do not use as fact

- KII and ZETA certificate registry pilot in the GCC region. No primary source
  reachable, secondary aggregation only, and enterprise consortium activity
  rather than anything re-derivable from L1 bytes. Ecosystem context at best.
- Two Substack posts surfaced in search, one claiming an imminent Binance
  listing and one alleging fraud. Neither opened, neither verified, both read
  as anonymous sensationalism. Existence noted, no claim repeated.
- A Michael Sutton post referencing a Silverscript audit nearing completion and
  Argent having a new home. X returned 402, date unconfirmed. Do not date it
  into the window.
- IzioDev onboarding to the Kaspa Eco Foundation. Likely predates the window.

  Message history needs a logged-in client. Not attempted: logging into the
  owner's account is out of bounds. If this source matters, it needs an export
  or a paste. This is a gap, not a finding of nothing.

  several posts and were not invented.
