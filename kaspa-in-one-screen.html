Toccata status

Real-time Proof of Work.

Kaspa keeps mined money and UTXO ownership, then changes the shape of the ledger. Instead of forcing every honest block into one slow line, it uses a blockDAG and GHOSTDAG so parallel honest work can stay in the record and still be ordered.

Kaspa is live as Proof of Work, UTXO ownership, GHOSTDAG, a blockDAG, and the Crescendo-era 10 BPS network. Wallets, explorers, nodes, mining, transfers, receipts, and accepted-transaction checks exist today. Toccata, native app rules, vProgs, and DAGKnight need separate activation evidence; the May 2026 TN10 activation and hardening tests are testnet evidence, not mainnet activation.

The short version.

Kaspa is a mined UTXO network trying to make Proof-of-Work payments feel closer to real time. It is interesting because it tests whether Bitcoin-style mining can support lower-latency usage without becoming a normal centralized fast database.

What is live: PoW mining, UTXO ownership, blockDAG structure, GHOSTDAG ordering, 10 BPS mainnet, wallets, explorers, nodes, mining, transfers, receipts, and accepted-transaction checks.

What is not live: Toccata mainnet activation, full vProgs, DAGKnight, native DeFi, TangVM/oracle systems, and full app-to-app atomic composition.

The short read.

Bitcoin-style mining culture, seconds-scale user feel, blockDAG ordering, verifiable receipts today, and future apps that prove their own rules.

Transaction payloads and accepted-transaction checks are live L1 evidence paths. Toccata, native assets, and vProgs remain separate protocol and roadmap work.
