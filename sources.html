Source hierarchy

What settles a claim

TierWhat it settlesWhere that is
1. Code, KIPs, releases Whether a feature exists, and when it turned on. rusty-kaspa, the KIP repo, release tags
2. A mainnet node or public API What a number is right now, with the moment it was read. api.kaspa.org, the block explorer
3. Papers, docs, interviews Nothing on its own. It explains why something was designed the way it was. Kaspa Research, docs.kaspa.org, HashdagKaspa co-founder Yonatan Sompolinsky's research writing site, where he lays out design ideas ahead of protocol work.
4. Posts and dashboards Nothing. They point at something else worth checking. X accounts, aggregators, community recaps

Nothing on this site counts as shipped on tier 3 or 4 alone. A number without a read date is treated the same way.

Excluded on purpose

Why kaspa.org's marketing pages are not a source

Kaspa.org, Lore, Build, and HODL stayed roughly eight weeks stale on Toccata's mainnet activation, checked twice, on 2026-07-11 and again on 2026-08-22. A docs page that wrong on the biggest protocol event of the year is not orientation. wiki.kaspa.org and docs.kaspa.org are separate subdomains and stay in use; only the marketing pages are excluded.

Community forums, social mirrors, and builder accounts to watch

kas-smiths.org, the KCC forum, feeds kaspanet/kccs and stays proposal-stage until merged as a KIP. kaspa.social mirrors Kaspa-related X posts as a scrape, not a curated feed: verify any claim against the original account. KASmedia (kasmagazine.com) runs recaps, interviews, and community context.
