Kaspa vs Solana for builders

Solana is the better choice for most production app categories today: it has the execution maturity, liquidity, and users already in place. Kaspa fits a narrower case, one where the product specifically needs fast mined ordering, self-custodied KAS flows, public receipts, or Toccata's covenant-style spend rules.

Status: payments are live today. Toccata activated at DAA score 474,165,565 on Rusty Kaspa v2.0.1. Wallet support, tooling, and app receipts for the new rule surface are still catching up.

Kaspa builder lane

Toccata activated the L1 rule surface at DAA score 474,165,565, and Rusty Kaspa v2.0.1 is the current stable release running on top of it. The base protocol work is done: covenants, ZK proof checks, and sequencing commitments are live rules the network enforces today. What's not done is everything that sits on top of that rule surface. Based-app work belongs to a separate roadmap and needs its own wallet, tooling, and transaction evidence before "live" applies to it too. Until those pieces catch up, the working Kaspa path is what already ships: wallets, receipts, accepted-transaction evidence, node reads, UTXO tracking.

Founder wording

Name the specific reason the product needs fast mined ordering or spend rules. "Kaspa is fast" tells a reader nothing; "the vault needs a spend cap enforced at the protocol layer" does. Porting a Solana app category onto Kaspa without a liquidity plan, wallet support, or transaction evidence is a pitch. It becomes a product once those three exist.
