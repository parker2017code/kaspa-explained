Kaspa coordination markets.

Kaspa coordination markets let people commit to an action only when enough compatible commitments exist. Kaspa fits when fast mined ordering, public commitments, and spend rules change what people can trust.

Timing note: payments are live today. New app-layer rules need Toccata activation and supporting wallets or tools.

Kaspa coordination markets product shape.

The simple pattern is: a user commits, the app groups compatible commitments, a transparent rule checks whether conditions are met, and funds release or refund.

The first useful versions can be public and replayable. Privacy, reusable capital, solver incentives, oracle markets, and atomic execution are harder design work.

Where it can fit.

Assurance funding

Software, research, events, local infrastructure, or public goods funded after a threshold.

Hackathon bounties

Prizes release when objective evidence or judge approval meets the published rule.

Resource auctions

Compute, data, access, or service capacity sold through visible commitments.

AI-agent tasks

Deposits, completion proofs, release/refund paths, and dispute evidence for machine-readable work.
