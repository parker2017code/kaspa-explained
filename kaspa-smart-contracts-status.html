What is live?

High-intent claim

Does Kaspa have smart contracts?

Not as a mature live Ethereum-style smart-contract environment. Kaspa has live payments and ecosystem KRC tooling today. Toccata targets covenant-style L1 programmability, and vProgs remain later roadmap architecture.

Citable summary: Kaspa's live network supports payments and ecosystem KRC tooling; Toccata and vProgs are the app-programmability path, not already-live native DeFi.

Safe wording

Copy this instead.

Kaspa does not currently have a mature live Ethereum-style smart-contract environment. Its near-term app path is Toccata: covenant-style spending rules, asset rules, Silverscript, ZK proof checks, and sequencing commitments, with richer vProgs architecture later.
What people overstate

KRC tokens prove native smart contracts are live.

What is true

KRC tooling can support real token workflows, but it is ecosystem tooling around Kaspa data.

What to check

Activation evidence, wallet support, indexers, liquidity, source of funds, and user-facing product evidence.
