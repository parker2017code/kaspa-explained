Toccata status

Status check

Does Kaspa have smart contracts?

Yes, as of DAA score 474,165,565: Toccata activated covenant rules, covenant IDs, ZK proof checks, and sequencing commitments directly on L1. Native DeFi, audited apps, and broad wallet support are separate claims that still need their own evidence.

Summary: Payments have run on Kaspa since mainnet launch. Toccata is the programmability upgrade. vProgs and native L1 DeFi come later, on their own timelines.
