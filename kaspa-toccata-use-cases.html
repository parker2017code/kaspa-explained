Kaspa Toccata use cases

Every Toccata use case reduces to four primitives: covenant-style L1 rules, ZK proof hooks, sequencing commitments, and based-app foundations. The protocol part is done; the wallets, indexers, and apps that turn a rule into something your users can touch are still building.

Status: Toccata is live on mainnet, activated at DAA 474,165,565 on Rusty Kaspa v2.0.1. Wallet, explorer, and app support for the new rules are still catching up.

Kaspa Toccata use cases source boundary

Rusty Kaspa v2.0.1 (June 15, 2026) is the current stable release. It builds on v2.0.0 (June 5, 2026), which carried the activation itself at DAA score 474,165,565. Kaspa.org Build's TN12 references and the KIP-17 TN10 notes describe the testnet work that preceded activation and read as history now. Argent adds early Silverscript research and tooling context; the merged ZK SDK work improves proof-script ergonomics for developers building on the now-live rule set.

Check: Kaspa.org Build, v2.0.1 release, v2.0.0 activation score, KIP-17, ZK SDK PR, Argent, and local status.

Builder rule

Lead with the product action: lock funds, cap a spend, issue an asset, refund a buyer, group commitments, verify a proof, replay app state. Name the Toccata primitive second. Your reader can evaluate "the vault enforces a 48-hour withdrawal delay" without knowing what a covenant is.
