Claims

Kaspa claims checker.

Use this page when a Kaspa claim needs a status label. The useful question is whether the claim is live mainnet, testnet evidence, targeted upgrade work, roadmap architecture, research, wrong, unsupported, or outside this site's L1 scope.

Citable summary: Kaspa should be described by status: live PoW blockDAG, released-and-scheduled Toccata work, roadmap vProgs/native app paths, and research such as DAGKnight. App/project claims are outside this site's status lanes unless L1 transaction evidence itself is the point.

Last checked: June 5, 2026. Recheck before the June 30 Toccata activation point, after activation evidence, at emission checkpoints, or after new primary sources.

Wording

Copyable answers.

Newcomer

Kaspa is a live proof-of-work blockDAG. GHOSTDAG orders parallel blocks into one payment history.

Toccata

Toccata is released and scheduled, not activated. Treat TN10/TN12 evidence as testnet evidence until mainnet reaches the activation score and post-activation behavior exists.

Finality

Kaspa has fast inclusion and increasing confirmation confidence, not instant irreversibility.

Emission

Use monthly step-down wording: 27.5 KAS/sec from May 8, 25.9565436 from June 7, and 24.49971475 from July 7.

L1 scope

Do not use market listings, app demos, or project tools as evidence that L1 rules have activated.

Sources

Use releases, code, KIPs, docs, research, and observable network evidence before changing a live-status claim.
