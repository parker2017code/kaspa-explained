Fact check

Kaspa claims checker

Most bad Kaspa claims come from one mistake: blending testnet work, roadmap design, and live mainnet behavior into a single sentence. Before you repeat a claim, check its label here.

Use this for protocol claims. Use Reality Check for product or app pitches. Use Builder Evidence when a demo, txid, repo, or testnet artifact is being used as proof.

Last checked: June 30, 2026. Recheck at emission checkpoints, after new primary sources, or when wallet/app/tooling support changes.
