What is live?

Claims

Kaspa claims checker.

Use this page when a Kaspa claim needs a status label. The useful question is whether the claim is live mainnet, ecosystem tooling, targeted upgrade work, roadmap architecture, research, or unsupported.

Citable summary: Kaspa should be described by status: live PoW blockDAG, ecosystem KRC tooling, targeted Toccata work, roadmap vProgs/native app paths, and research such as DAGKnight or RTD-derived systems.

Wording

Copyable answers.

Newcomer

Kaspa is a live proof-of-work blockDAG. GHOSTDAG orders parallel blocks into one payment history.

Toccata

Toccata is the targeted hard-fork track for covenant-style programmability. Treat it as pending until mainnet activation evidence exists.

Finality

Kaspa has fast inclusion and increasing confirmation confidence, not instant irreversibility.

KRC

KRC standards can support ecosystem token workflows today, but they are not native smart contracts or vProgs.

Apps

A useful Kaspa app still needs users, wallet flow, indexer/source-of-truth choices, funding, and proof that the app did what it claimed.
