Kaspa vs Ethereum for apps.

Kaspa vs Ethereum is a maturity and settlement tradeoff. Ethereum has the mature smart-contract ecosystem; Kaspa has a live PoW blockDAG payment layer and a targeted path toward covenant rules, proof hooks, sequencing commitments, and later app programs.

Status boundary: use current status and Toccata status before calling any app-layer capability live.

Kaspa vs Ethereum by app maturity.

Use Ethereum or its L2 ecosystem when the product needs deployed smart contracts, composable DeFi, stablecoin liquidity, audited toolchains, existing wallets, and mature indexers today.

Where Kaspa may be worth building around.

Fast PoW payment feel

Wallets and receipts can use the live network today.

UTXO rule design

Vault, escrow, asset, and funding rules can map naturally to constrained outputs once Toccata activates.

Shared sequencing thesis

Based apps and later vProgs point toward app logic that shares Kaspa ordering and proof surfaces.

Do not copy the category.

A Kaspa version of lending, markets, launch tools, or stable assets needs its own custody model, oracle assumptions, liquidity, wallet path, and proof of accepted behavior. A name from Ethereum does not make a Kaspa product ready.
