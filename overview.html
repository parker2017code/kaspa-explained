Kaspa in 90 seconds

Kaspa keeps Bitcoin-style mining but lets honest blocks land in parallel instead of racing for one slot. GHOSTDAG then orders that graph into a single shared payment history.

For shipped-versus-roadmap detail, use the status table.

The short version

What to know first

What Kaspa is

Proof of Work, UTXO, blockDAG, GHOSTDAG. The core move is mined ordering that doesn't throw away competing blocks.

What is live

Mainnet PoW, UTXOs, GHOSTDAG ordering, pruning-oriented node operation, and the 10 BPS Crescendo-era network.

What comes next

Toccata is live, putting covenant and proof-check primitives on mainnet. vProgs and native DeFi are roadmap. DAGKnight and RTD-derived app systems are research or future architecture.

Design case

Kaspa is testing whether Bitcoin-style mining can feel fast enough for everyday use while L1 stays focused on ordering, ownership, and proofs. App rules build on that same base.
