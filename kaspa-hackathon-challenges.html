Kaspa hackathon ideas

A hackathon demo is worthless the moment the room empties unless it leaves evidence behind: a working artifact, a source list, a status label, a result a reviewer can inspect cold.

Status: payments are live today. Toccata app-layer rules need wallet support, tooling support, and app receipts.

Hackathon tracks

AI-agent escrow

Agent posts a task, worker submits proof, wallet releases or refunds against a rule the reader can inspect, not agent logic hidden in a backend.

Receipt wallet

A receipt page with txid, accepted state, source, amount, and a fallback verification path when the primary one fails.

Founder fit scanner

Turns a product pitch into six fields: user, job, evidence, status, risk, next action. No pitch survives that unchanged.

Vault policy UX

Shows a spend cap, delay, recovery path, and a rejected spend side by side, in language a non-engineer can read.

Node/API comparison

Runs one transaction against a hosted API, a public node, an explorer, and a local node, then shows where they disagree.

Assurance bounty

Commits funds to a project only after enough participants join by a deadline. Miss it, and the funds never move.
