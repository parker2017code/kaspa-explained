Kaspa hackathon ideas.

Kaspa hackathon ideas should produce a working artifact, a source trail, and a status label. The goal is builder learning and useful evidence, not a generic token demo.

Status boundary: use current status and Toccata status before calling any app-layer capability live.

Kaspa hackathon ideas challenge tracks.

AI-agent escrow

Agent posts task, worker submits proof, wallet releases or refunds through a visible rule.

Receipt wallet

Generate a receipt page with txid, accepted state, source, amount, and fallback verification.

Founder fit scanner

Turn a product pitch into user, job, evidence, status, risk, and next action.

Vault policy UX

Show a spend cap, delay, recovery path, and rejected spend in a user-readable flow.

Node/API comparison

Compare hosted API, public node, explorer, and local node status for one transaction.

Assurance bounty

Commit funds to a project only after enough participants join under a deadline.

Post-event pipeline.
