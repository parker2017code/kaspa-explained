Reality check

Test the pitch before the narrative

A protocol activating on schedule doesn't tell you whether an app built on it works. Product claims need a different test: who uses it, where the liquidity comes from, who signs what, and what evidence backs it up.

Use this for app and product claims. For protocol status, use the claims checker. When a demo or txid is being used to prove a builder claim, use builder evidence.

The part no scan reaches

What a keyword check cannot tell you

Both tables above check whether a pitch names the parts a reviewer looks for. Neither tells you the product is worth building. That answer comes from naming the person who would use it, the step they currently do some other way, and what they give up by switching. A pitch that survives that question, with a status label on every claim inside it, is one worth posting.
